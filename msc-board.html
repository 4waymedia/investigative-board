How to use this board
✕

Build an investigative map of events, people, and connections:

  • Drag cards from the bottom tray onto the board to create card pins.
  • Click on empty space to drop a free pin in the current color & type.
  • Sequential string: each new pin auto-connects from the last one, with a wiggly “string”.
  • Shift + click two pins to create a direct connection between them.
  • Click a line (Pin tool) to insert a new pin and split that connection.
  • Alt / ⌘ + click on a line to delete that connection.
  • Drag a pin to move it. Shift + drag moves its whole connected cluster.
  • Scroll to pan. Ctrl / ⌘ / Alt + scroll to zoom in and out.
  • Double-click a pin to edit its title, color, notes, and date.
  • Click ⏳ Auto Timeline to lay dated pins out along a time axis.
  • Click ★ Magic Place to auto-place unused cards in a spiral constellation as card pins.

Shortcuts: P = Pin tool, M = Move tool, Ctrl/Cmd+Z = Undo, Delete = delete selected pin.
